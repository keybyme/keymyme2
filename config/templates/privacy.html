What KeyByMe is

KeyByMe (keybyme.com) is a personal organizer app: it lets an account holder keep contacts, passwords, bookmarked links, documents/photos/videos, and reminders in one place, organized by category. Accounts are created or approved by the site administrator; each account only ever sees its own data.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, either entered directly or provided by Google when you sign in with your Google account.
  • Content you store: contacts, passwords, links, files, and reminders you add to your own account.
  • Basic usage data: how much of your storage quota is in use, and timestamps such as when a record was created or updated.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google," we request your name, email address, and profile picture from Google to create or log you into your KeyByMe account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google data, and we never post to any Google service on your behalf.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate your account: to show you your own contacts/passwords/links/files/reminders, to enforce your storage quota, and to email you reminder notifications you've scheduled (sent via our email provider). We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How your information is protected

Passwords you store in KeyByMe are encrypted before being saved to the database. Your session is protected by a login cookie. Access to the underlying server is limited to the site administrator.

Who we share information with

We don't share your information with third parties, except the minimum needed to run the service: Google (if you sign in with Google) and our email provider (to deliver reminder emails you scheduled). We do not sell your data.

Cookies

KeyByMe uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any record you've added at any time from within the app. To close your account entirely or request deletion of your data, contact the administrator at the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to wesnetworking@gmail.com.
